1. Pre-engagement and Planning
Determine the necessity and scope of testing:

Write down the scope and target of your penetration test
Document what location, facility, or specific goal you will target
Get written permission and create a contract before proceeding
Ensure appropriate sign-off from corporate executives 234
As emphasized in Syngress IT Security Project Management Handbook, "The last thing you need is for you or one of your staff to be falsely accused of attacking the network when, in fact, you were legitimately testing the security of the network".4

2. Intelligence Gathering and Reconnaissance
Information gathering activities include:

Document the facility's location and access controls
Identify location and coverage of external cameras
Map primary and secondary entrances and exits
Note guard positions and externally visible security details
Use cameras and satellite views to create security control maps 23
3. Threat Modeling and Vulnerability Analysis
Think like an attacker:

Determine how attackers would most likely try to attack your network
Locate vulnerabilities and areas of weakness across the entire enterprise
Use publicly available information as a starting point 4
4. Exploitation Phase
Profile and exploit systems:

Profile external systems and applications
Identify potential architectural weaknesses
Identify exploitable vulnerabilities
Exploit weaknesses while maintaining ethical boundaries 5
5. Post-Exploitation
Document and analyze:

Determine if attacks can be detected in real-time or retrospectively
Analyze the attack footprint and target size
Document findings on the way to your target 4
6. Reporting
Create a comprehensive risk mitigation plan (RMP) that:

Documents all actions taken during the test
Includes results, interpretations, and recommendations
Provides clear remediation strategies 1
7. Cleanup
Remove any changes made during the testing process to restore systems to their original state.1

AI Enhancement for Penetration Testing
AI-Powered Automation
Modern AI tools can significantly enhance penetration testing workflows. As described in Bash Shell Scripting for Pentesters, AI technologies offer new capabilities for both defenders and attackers.6

AI applications in pentesting include:

Automated vulnerability identification: AI can process large volumes of pentest data and feed it into models for analysis
Decision-making support: AI systems can guide testing strategies and prioritize efforts
Data analysis automation: AI can analyze attack surfaces and potential threat actors 67
